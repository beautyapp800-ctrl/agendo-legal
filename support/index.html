What is Agendo

Agendo is a personal appointment diary for beauty professionals. Everything stays on your phone: no accounts, no servers, no data collection.

Contact

Questions and requests: beautyapp800@gmail.com. We will reply as soon as we can.

Restore purchases

Open the app, then Settings → “Subscription” → “Restore purchases”. Use the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with.

Cancel a subscription

Subscriptions are managed by the store, not by us. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

How to cancel on Android (Google Play)

Export your data

Open the app, then Settings → “Export and import” → “Export to a file”. Your data is never deleted, even without a subscription, and you can always export it.
