Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 6, 2026
Developer: Eduard Bakulin, beautyapp800@gmail.com

In short

Agendo collects no data about you. There are no accounts, no sign-in and no server. Everything you type stays on your device.

What you enter

Client names, phone numbers, service names, prices, dates, times and notes. All of it lives in a database file inside the app on your phone. We have no access to it and cannot read it.

What the app does NOT do

It sends nothing over the network. It has no analytics, advertising, trackers or crash reporting. It never asks for your camera, photos, contacts or location. The only permission it may request is notifications, so it can remind you about an appointment.

Reminders

Reminders are created and shown by your phone itself. They never pass through a server and they reach only you, never your clients.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by the Apple App Store and Google Play. They process payment under their own privacy policies; we never see your payment details. The app only asks the store whether you hold an active purchase.

Backups and export

The database file is included in your phone’s normal backup (iCloud or Google) if you have one switched on, under Apple’s or Google’s terms. When you export your data to a file, it is saved only where you send it.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device. Because we store nothing, there is no need to ask us to delete anything.

Children

The app is intended for professional use by adults and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated text ships with a new version of the app. Questions: beautyapp800@gmail.com
